What We Move

Specialty items, handled by specialists

Every piece gets a plan before it gets a truck. We survey the item, the doorways, the stairs and the destination, then build the protection around it.

Pianos

Uprights, baby grands and concert grands. Leg and lyre removal, board and skid work, stair and crane access, plus tuning referrals once the instrument has settled.

Paintings & Fine Art

Glassine and acid-free wrapping, mirror cartons, corner protection and custom art crates. Sculpture, statuary and gallery installs handled with the same care.

Grandfather Clocks

Weights, pendulum, chime rods and cables secured and labelled by hand, the case blanket-wrapped and braced upright, then reassembled and levelled on arrival.

Armoires & Antiques

Highboys, secretaries, curio cabinets and hutches. Doors and drawers immobilised, veneer and marble protected, hardware bagged and tagged for reassembly.

New Furniture Delivery

Receiving, inspection, deluxing and final-mile delivery for designers, showrooms and homeowners. We unbox, assemble, place and take the packaging away with us.

Safes & Heavy Equipment

Gun safes, floor safes, vaults and commercial equipment. Stair-climbing gear, rigging and floor protection, with anchoring at the destination when required.

Valuables & Collections

Jewellery, silver, coin and wine collections, memorabilia and documents. Sealed inventory, restricted access and direct point-to-point transport.

Custom Crating

Built on site to the millimetre when a blanket and a strap aren't enough — for chandeliers, marble, statuary, oversized canvases and anything irreplaceable.

Local & Long Distance

Across town or across the country, on a dedicated truck. Your shipment is not co-loaded with someone else's household goods and does not sit in a warehouse.

How It Works

Four steps, no surprises

Survey & Quote

Tell us the item and the route by phone or photo. For larger jobs we walk the property, measure the exits and give you a written, itemised price.

Protection Plan

We choose the materials and the method for each piece — crate, skid, board, blanket, rigging — and schedule the crew and equipment it needs.

Precision Transport

Floors and door jambs protected, everything inventoried out and secured in an air-ride truck. One crew stays with your shipment the whole way.

Placement & Sign-off

Unwrapped, reassembled, levelled and placed exactly where you want it. Debris removed, inventory checked against the manifest, then you sign.
