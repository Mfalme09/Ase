Apartment Moves

Specialty Items

Fine art, safes and pianos, handled by specialists

Some things can't be replaced, so they can't be mishandled. Every specialty piece gets a plan before it gets a truck.

Instruments

Piano Moving

Spinets and uprights, baby grands, parlor grands and concert instruments, plus organs and antique player pianos.

  • Preparation. The lid and fallboard are secured, the lyre and legs removed on grands, and every fitting is bagged, tagged and travels with the instrument.
  • Support. Grands are strapped to a padded piano board and skid; uprights are covered edge to edge and moved on purpose-built dollies.
  • Access. Stairs, tight turns and balconies planned for. We measure before the truck is booked.
  • After delivery. Reassembled, leveled and positioned away from vents and direct sun.
Get an estimate

Fine Art

Fine Art, Antiques & Heirlooms

Paintings, framed works, mirrors, sculpture, grandfather clocks and antique furniture for collectors, galleries, designers and families.

  • Wrapping. Acid-free tissue against the surface, foam corners on the frame, then a rigid outer layer. Nothing abrasive ever touches paint or gilding.
  • Transport. Works travel upright and braced, never flat under weight.
  • Clocks & antiques. Clock weights, pendulums and chimes secured; armoires and case goods taken apart only where they were built to come apart.
  • Installation. Hanging, leveling and securing at the destination, including heavy mirrors.
