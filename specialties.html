Estate Sales

Handled with care, for the family as much as the furniture

Settling an estate is rarely just logistics. We work quietly and patiently alongside families, executors, attorneys, trustees and estate sale companies — and we treat a grandmother's china the way we would treat a Steinway.

Whether the property is being sold, divided among heirs or emptied to a deadline, you get one point of contact and one plan for the whole house.

Office Moves

Close Friday. Open Monday.

An office relocation is measured in downtime. We plan around your calendar, move after hours where it helps, and label to a floor plan so people arrive to a desk that is already theirs — monitor, chair, files and all.

Single suites, multi-floor headquarters, medical and dental practices, law firms, warehouses and retail locations. We coordinate with building management, reserve the freight elevator and protect the common areas so move day runs to the schedule you were promised.

Liquidations

Assets converted, buildings cleared, everything on the record

Closures, downsizings, lease expirations, bankruptcies and portfolio consolidations. When assets have to be inventoried, moved and turned into something other than a storage bill, we handle the physical side end to end.

We work with business owners, property managers, attorneys, trustees and auctioneers, and we leave you with the documentation your accountants and your court filings need.
